## Overview
Security and Risk Management is the first domain of CISSP, focused on establishing overall security governance, defining organizational security goals, managing risk, ensuring legal/regulatory compliance, and maintaining business continuity plans.

## Key Mechanics
* **Governance & Strategy:** Aligning security objectives with organizational strategy and policy.
* **Compliance & Legal:** Adhering to relevant laws, regulatory mandates, and industry compliance requirements.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Identifying, analyzing, and reducing threats to acceptable organizational thresholds.
