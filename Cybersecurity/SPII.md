## Overview
Sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (SPII) is a high-risk subset of PII requiring stricter handling and encryption protocols due to the severe impact of exposure.

## Key Examples
* Social Security Numbers (SSN) or national identification numbers.
* Financial account credentials and credit card information.
* Medical/health records and biometric identification data.

## Security Impact & Context
* **Threat/Risk:** Theft of SPII significantly increases the risk of financial fraud and long-term [[Identity theft]].
* **Mitigation/Defense:** Enforce zero-trust access limits, robust data-at-rest/data-in-transit encryption, and strict regulatory compliance (e.g., [[GDPR]]).
