## Overview
The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a comprehensive data protection and privacy regulation established by the European Union to give individuals greater control over their personal data.

## Key Mechanics & Context
* **Scope:** Regulates how organizations collect, process, store, and destroy [[PII]] belonging to EU residents, regardless of where the organization is located.
* **Core Rights:** Includes rights such as the right to access, correct, and request the erasure ("right to be forgotten") of personal data.
* **Compliance Impact:** Non-compliance can result in heavy regulatory fines, audits, and legal liabilities.
