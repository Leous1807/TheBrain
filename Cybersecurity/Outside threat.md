## Threat Description
An outside threat is a threat actor operating from outside the perimeter of an organization with no authorized access to its internal systems or networks.

## Execution Vectors
* **Network Exploitation:** Scanning for public-facing vulnerabilities, unpatched software, or misconfigured firewalls.
* **Social Engineering:** Targeting employees through [[Phishing]] or pretexting to harvest credentials or deploy [[Malware]].

## Prevention & Remediation
* **Defenses:** Perimeter [[Firewall]] deployment, intrusion detection systems (IDS), robust external authentication (MFA), and patch management.
