# Description
In 2017 attackers successfully infiltrated the credit reporting company, Equifax. The attack resulted in one of the greatest data breaches in history. Attackers successfully gained access to over 143 million customer records. The attack happened due to several mistakes by the company. The Equifax eventually settled with the US government for over 500 million dollars. This attack alerted companies of the financial impact of a data breach and the importance of security.
