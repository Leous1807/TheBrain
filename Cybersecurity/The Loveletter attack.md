# Description
In the year 2000 this malware was created to steal credential login information. The victim would receive an email titled "Love Letter", inside the email there was an attachment link labeled "A love letter for you". Once the victim opened the link, the malware would scan the computers address book and send itself to every person on the list. This attack was the first example of social engineering.
