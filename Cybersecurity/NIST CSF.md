## Overview
The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) is a voluntary guideline consisting of standards, industry practices, and recommendations to help organizations manage and reduce cybersecurity risks.

## Core Functions
* **Identify:** Understand organizational assets, risks, and security context.
* **Protect:** Implement safeguards to ensure continuous delivery of critical services.
* **Detect:** Develop mechanisms to identify the occurrence of a cybersecurity event.
* **Respond:** Formulate procedures to take action upon detecting a security incident.
* **Recover:** Maintain plans for resilience and restoration of capabilities impaired by an incident.
