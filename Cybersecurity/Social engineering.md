## Threat Description
Social engineering is a psychological manipulation technique that exploits human trust and human error to trick individuals into disclosing confidential information or granting unauthorized access to physical or digital assets.

## Key Vectors
* **[[Phishing]]:** Using fraudulent digital messages to harvest credentials or deploy malicious payloads.
* **Pretexting:** Inventing scenarios to convince victims to release information.
* **Baiting / Tailgating:** Leveraging physical or digital offers (or physical unauthorized entry) to breach barriers.

## Prevention & Remediation
* **User Awareness:** Security awareness training programs.
* **Verification Protocols:** Establishing strict procedures for identity verification during data requests.
