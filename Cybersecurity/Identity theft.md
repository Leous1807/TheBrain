## Overview
Identity theft is the illegal act of acquiring and using someone else's personal data—typically without their knowledge—to commit fraud or impersonate the victim.

## Execution Path & Attack Context
* **Initial Access:** Often achieved through social engineering (e.g., [[Phishing]]), credential harvesting, or large-scale data compromise (e.g., [[The Equifax breach]]).
* **Exploitation:** Malicious actors utilize stolen [[PII]] or [[SPII]] to open unauthorized financial accounts, execute fraudulent transactions, or gain illegal access to systems.

## Prevention & Remediation
* **Detection:** Monitoring financial statements, credit reports, and dark web credential leaks.
* **Prevention:** Utilizing strong authentication methods, safeguarding [[PII]]/[[SPII]], and exercising caution against digital communication scams.
