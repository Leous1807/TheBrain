## Threat Description
Phishing is a social engineering attack that uses digital communications (e.g., email, SMS, messaging apps) to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information (such as credentials or [[PII]]) or downloading [[Malware]].

## Common Variants
* **Spear Phishing:** Highly targeted phishing campaigns directed at specific individuals or organizations.
* **Whaling:** Phishing aimed specifically at high-profile executives.
* **Vishing / Smishing:** Phishing conducted via voice calls (Vishing) or SMS text messages (Smishing).

## Prevention & Remediation
* **Technical Controls:** Email filtering systems (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), Web Application Firewalls.
* **Administrative Controls:** Security awareness training, phishing simulation exercises, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
