## Overview
Asset Security is the CISSP domain focused on protecting physical and digital assets throughout their lifecycle, including data classification, ownership, retention, and secure destruction.

## Key Mechanics / Lifecycle Management
* **Classification & Ownership:** Categorizing data based on sensitivity and assigning data owners.
* **Storage Maintenance:** Implementing access restrictions and encryption for data at rest and in transit.
* **Retention & Destruction:** Establishing data retention policies and enforcing sanitized data destruction methods (e.g., degaussing, shredding, cryptographic erasure).
