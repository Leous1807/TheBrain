## Overview
The security lifecycle is an evolving cycle of standards, policies, and continuous processes that dictate how an organization manages risk, adheres to internal guidelines, and maintains regulatory compliance over time.

## Key Attributes
* **Continuous Evolution:** Security policies must adapt dynamically as new threats, technologies, and regulatory requirements emerge.
* **Governance Alignment:** Ensures ongoing compliance with relevant laws, standards (e.g., [[GDPR]]), and internal benchmarks.
