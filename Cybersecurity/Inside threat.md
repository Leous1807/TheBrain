## Threat Description
An insider threat is a threat actor who originates from within an organization. This includes current or former employees, trusted contractors, or third-party business partners who have authorized access to internal systems and data.

## Threat Profiles
* **Malicious Insider:** Intentionally abuses legitimate credentials to exfiltrate data, sabotage systems, or steal intellectual property.
* **Negligent Insider:** Unintentionally compromises security through poor habits, such as falling for [[Phishing]] or misconfiguring cloud storage.

## Prevention & Remediation
* **Prevention:** Principle of Least Privilege, strict [[Identity and access management]] policies, and offboarding protocols.
* **Detection:** User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA), data loss prevention (DLP) controls, and continuous audit logging.
