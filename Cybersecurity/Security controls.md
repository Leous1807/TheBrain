## Overview
Security controls are safeguards or countermeasures designed to preserve the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and systems by mitigating specific security risks.

## Control Categories
* **Technical Controls:** Automated mechanisms implemented in hardware/software (e.g., [[Firewall]], encryption).
* **Administrative Controls:** Policy-based guidelines, training programs, and operational standards.
* **Physical Controls:** Physical barriers protecting facilities and hardware (e.g., locks, security guards, badge readers).
