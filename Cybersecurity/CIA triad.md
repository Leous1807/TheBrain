## Overview
The CIA triad is a foundational security model designed to guide organizational policies, controls, and risk management assessments for systems and data assets.

## Key Mechanics / Core Components
* **Confidentiality:** Guarantees that sensitive data and [[Asset|assets]] are accessible only to authorized entities.
* **Integrity:** Ensures data remains authentic, accurate, complete, and uncorrupted by unauthorized modification or deletion.
* **Availability:** Guarantees that systems, networks, and data remain operational and accessible to authorized users when needed.

## Security Impact & Context
* **Threat/Risk:** Flaws in any pillar lead to security incidents (e.g., data breaches compromise confidentiality; ransomware compromises availability).
* **Mitigation/Defense:** Implemented through technical, administrative, and physical [[Security controls]] (e.g., encryption for confidentiality, backups for availability, hashing for integrity).
