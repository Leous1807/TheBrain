## Overview
A threat actor is an individual, group, or organization that intentionally or unintentionally executes malicious or damaging activities against digital assets, networks, or personnel.

## Primary Classifications
* **[[Inside threat]]:** Current/former employees, contractors, or business partners with authorized system credentials.
* **[[Outside threat]]:** External entities operating outside organizational boundaries (e.g., cybercriminals, hacktivists, nation-states).
