## Overview
Personally Identifiable Information (PII) refers to any information that can be used alone or combined with other data to identify a specific individual.

## Examples & Categories
* **Direct Identifiers:** Full legal name, address, email address, national identification numbers.
* **Indirect Identifiers:** Date of birth, IP address, device IDs, telephone numbers.

## Security Impact & Context
* **Threat/Risk:** PII is a primary target for threat actors during data breaches, leading to financial fraud and [[Identity theft]].
* **Mitigation/Defense:** Managed through strict access controls, encryption at rest/in transit, and compliance frameworks such as [[GDPR]].
