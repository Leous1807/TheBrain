## Overview
Transferable skills are non-technical foundational competencies acquired from previous academic, personal, or professional experiences that apply across cybersecurity roles.

## Essential Transferable Skills
* **Communication:** Effectively conveying complex technical risks to non-technical stakeholders.
* **Collaboration:** Working effectively within multidisciplinary IT, legal, and operational teams.
* **Analytical Thinking:** Evaluating complex log data to deduce root causes.
* **Problem Solving:** Designing creative workarounds or immediate containment strategies during live incidents.
