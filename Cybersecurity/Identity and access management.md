## Overview
Identity and Access Management (IAM) is a framework of business processes, policies, and technologies that ensures authorized individuals have the appropriate access to physical assets (e.g., facilities) and logical assets (e.g., networks, applications, and data).

## Key Mechanics / Core Principles
* **Authentication (Who are you?):** Verifying identity via credentials, MFA, or biometrics.
* **Authorization (What can you do?):** Granting specific permissions based on the Principle of Least Privilege.
* **Accounting/Auditing:** Tracking user activity across physical and logical systems for accountability.

## Security Impact & Context
* **Threat/Risk:** Weak access management can lead to unauthorized data access, privilege escalation, or [[Identity theft]].
* **Mitigation/Defense:** Enforces role-based access controls (RBAC) and strict authentication policies.
